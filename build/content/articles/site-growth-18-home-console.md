# 建站成长记录 18：常去的地方，放在一起

首页导航在 HOME 旁新增了“控制台”。这是给访客使用的入口集合，不是站长后台，也不接入任何私密管理功能。

## 三个片段，一个位置

控制台里的三个入口分别通向奇想馆、碎片独处和创造纪事。去掉缩略图，使用编号、图标和按键，把空间留给明确的操作。

右下保留浮光集、友链、关于我的快捷访问。抽奖机区域先保留机位，并提供通向首页现有抽奖机的链接；这里没有另造一个抽奖流程。

## 日志单独滚动，控制台保持原位

整张控制台在屏幕中居中，不需要上下拖动才能看到按钮。左侧改成简短的更新日志，每条带日期，用一句话说明发生了什么。

当日志超出可用高度时，只滚动日志本身。手机上也保留独立的日志区域，不让它把右侧入口和底部操作挤出屏幕。

## 从游艺开始，下一次换个样子

四款主题最终都保留下来：

- **游艺**：粉、黄、薄荷色按键与清晰的复古边框。
- **夜航**：深色面板，搭配柔和的粉色与青绿色。
- **晴笺**：浅色纸面、细线与更轻的文字层次。
- **银钛**：银灰材质、金属按键与冷色细节。

首次打开默认游艺，之后依次轮换夜航、晴笺、银钛，再回到游艺。也可以手动选主题，下一次从所选主题继续轮换。

浏览器能保存偏好时，刷新后会接着轮换；存储不可用时，同一次页面访问里仍能正常切换。

## 小操作也要完整

底部保留音乐、主题、分享链接、回到顶部和收起。首页原有的封面切换继续独立工作。键盘可以在控制台里移动焦点，Escape 可以收起；主题选择器打开时，先关闭选择器。

这次没有改变原有页面的归属，只是让常用入口更容易找到。

# Site Growth Log 18: Keeping Familiar Places Together

A new Console button sits beside HOME. It is a visitor-facing collection of entrances, not the owner dashboard, and it has no private administration functions.

## Three fragments, one place

The three keys open Project Cosmos, the external blog, and the growth timeline. Numbered controls and icons replace thumbnails. Quick links lead to Glimmer, Friends, and About.

The lottery area reserves space for a future station while linking to the existing homepage machine. It does not introduce another draw workflow.

## The log scrolls, the console stays

The console remains centered with its controls visible. The left side is a brief, dated update log. Only this region scrolls when its contents exceed the available height, including on mobile.

## Arcade first, then a different material

All four designs remain: pastel tactile Arcade, dark Night, editorial Paper, and metallic Titanium.

The first opening uses Arcade. Later openings cycle through Night, Paper, Titanium, and back to Arcade. Manual selection remains available, and the next opening advances from that choice.

When browser storage is available, the rotation continues after a reload. When it is blocked, the current page session still rotates in memory.

## Complete controls

Music, theme selection, link sharing, back to top, and close stay on the bottom strip. The homepage cover switch remains independent. Keyboard focus stays inside the dialog, and Escape dismisses the theme picker before closing the console itself.

Existing pages keep their roles; the console simply makes them easier to reach.
