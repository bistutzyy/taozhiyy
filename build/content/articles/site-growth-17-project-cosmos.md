# 建站成长记录 17：把项目放进星空

片段 01 现在通向“奇想馆”。它不再只展示一个作品，而是收录自己做的、正在尝试的，以及值得分享的项目。

## 先看项目，再看出处

没有再把内容分成“我的作品”和“精选分享”。它们放在同一张项目目录里，用图文简介让人先判断是否感兴趣。

首批收录九个项目。进入详情后，可以继续查看具体介绍、使用教程、在线试用入口或试用效果。原作者和来源链接单独标清，第三方项目仍以原仓库的许可证和使用说明为准。

## 用滑动探索，不用翻页箭头

最初尝试过展厅式布局，后来改为星空。这里刻意避开主站四季的花、海、叶和雪，让项目分享页有自己的辨识度。

星空不靠分页箭头推进，滚动或触摸滑动就能看到后续项目。随后又调整了星光亮度和运动表现，保留空间感，同时让浏览更连贯。

## 入口与落款

首页片段 01 保留原来的视觉素材，与片段 02、03 使用一致的信息层级。项目页左上角的署名统一改为 **taozhiyo**。

这次把一条展示链接，变成了可以持续增加内容的项目入口。

# Site Growth Log 17: Projects Among the Stars

Fragment 01 now opens Project Cosmos. Instead of featuring just one piece of work, it gathers personal projects, experiments, and useful discoveries in one place.

## Projects first, credits always

Personal work and curated discoveries are no longer separate categories. Each project gets an image and a short introduction, followed by a detail view with practical instructions, demo links, or recorded trial results.

The first collection contains nine projects. Original authors and source links remain visible. Third-party projects retain their own licenses and usage requirements.

## Scroll instead of turning pages

An exhibition-like layout came first, but the final direction is a starfield. It avoids repeating the flowers, ocean, leaves, and snow already used by the four seasonal pages.

Scrolling or swiping reveals the next projects without pagination arrows. Star brightness and motion were then adjusted to make exploration brighter and smoother while preserving a sense of depth.

## A consistent entrance

The homepage keeps Fragment 01's visual asset while bringing its information treatment in line with Fragments 02 and 03. The project header now uses the name **taozhiyo**.

One showcase link has become a collection that can keep growing.
